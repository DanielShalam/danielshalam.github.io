Publications

WACV 2027

Propose and Attend: Training-free MLLM Grounding Confidence via Multi-Token Localized Attention

Daniel Shalam, Emanuel Ben Baruch, Avi Ben Cohen, Tal Remez

Scores an MLLM’s boxes and time windows by how strongly the prediction’s tokens attend inside the region they claim. Training-free, it improves hallucination AUROC by +8 to +21 over the best prior baseline across images, video and audio, and lifts the zero-shot COCO AP of an 8B MLLM from 20.4 to 36.5.

CVPR 2027 Under review

Text as Image: Image-Free Adaptation of Vision-Language Models through Their Visual Slots

Daniel Shalam, Avi Ben Cohen, Yochai Zvik, Emanuel Ben Baruch

Adapts a VLM to new categories from LLM-written class descriptions alone, by writing them into the prompt’s visual slots instead of the text. It beats training on the descriptions as text or as rendered images across three VLM families and nine image and video benchmarks, and the adapters transfer to video without seeing a single frame.

ICLR 2027 Under review

Adapt Part of the Way: Class Text as a Prior for Few-Shot Self-Supervised Vision

Daniel Shalam, Simon Korman

Gives self-supervised vision encoders, which have no text tower, the use of class names: a separate text encoder’s class embeddings are aligned into the visual space and transported only part of the way toward the support. +8.4 points over a DINOv3 linear probe at 1-shot on ImageNet, with no validation labels used.

ICML 2026 FM4LS Workshop

Few-Shot Biomedical Image Classification by Alignment of Independently Pretrained Encoders

Daniel Shalam, Simon Korman

Aligns independently pretrained vision and text encoders so class names guide few-shot classification in biomedical imaging, without a jointly trained vision-language model.

Unsupervised Representation Learning by Balanced Self-Attention Matching

Daniel Shalam, Simon Korman

Self-supervised learning that matches the self-attention distributions of augmented views to a balanced target from self-optimal transport, avoiding feature collapse without negative pairs, memory banks or asymmetric networks.

ICML 2024

The Balanced-Pairwise-Affinities Feature Transform

Daniel Shalam, Simon Korman

A parameter-free, differentiable feature transform that solves optimal transport of a feature set to itself and uses the transport plan as the new representation. State-of-the-art results in few-shot classification, unsupervised clustering and person re-identification.
